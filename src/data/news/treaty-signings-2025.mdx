Between August and September 2025, the Majagi Larrakia 1600 Countries Societatum of TIBNLA — alongside the Nations of the Pintupi Wati Tjuta Tjungarinyi — concluded a sustained wave of treaty agreements with fellow Tribal Nations across Terra Australis and with international partners.

All agreements are entered into in compliance with Articles 1, 3.1 and 7 of the Vienna Convention for Treaties between Nation States, and affirm the principle of **First in Time, Best at Lore/Law**.

## Domestic treaties

- **Kaurareg Nation, Kaiwalagal Plenum Dominium** — 14 August 2025
- **Nyamal Widajurra Country** (with Pintupi Wati Tjuta Tjungarinyi) — 23 August 2025
- **Adnyamathanha Tribal Nation** (with Pintupi Wati Tjuta Tjungarinyi) — 26 August 2025
- **Kunjen Olkol Country, Sea Hawk People** (with Pintupi Wati Tjuta Tjungarinyi) — 27 August 2025
- **Ni'ripono Gwata Tu Sovereign Government** (with Pintupi Wati Tjuta Tjungarinyi) — 19 September 2025

## International treaties

- **Subanen Tribe Acopiado Clan 1750**, Opirian Kingdom of the Philippine Islands — 12 August 2025
- **Vanua Ko Viti (Fiji)** — 23 August 2025: a Treaty of Cultural Recognition and Socio-Economic Development establishing a Joint Cultural Commission and a Bilateral Development Fund.

## What the treaties establish

Each treaty commits the parties to friendly relations and mutual respect for cultures, apical bloodline hierarchies, family histories, beliefs, and trade. They affirm adherence to each Nation's foundational rules-based system of cultural lore, and provide for cooperation in trade and barter, cultural exchange, education and training, the arts, tourism, and shared development opportunities.
